Have you ever wanted to have a history of what you copy to the clipboard for later use?
AI_CopyPaste allows:
Multiple items stored away...text, Images, URL's Files...simply select "share" (via Apples “Share” feature) and then select the AiCopyPaste extension, OR…whatever you have copied to clipboard add direct within the AI_CopyPaste app directly via the import button 

Then swap to your AiCopyPaste app and select any item you want returned immediately to your current clipboard for immediate use.

You can either select to store to iCloud and then have access to clipboard items amongst your devices, or keep your items stored on the local device only.

Device security locking via a PIN and use of Apples own biometrics to ensure no unauthorized person is looking at any clippings.

Pin your favorite clippings, by swiping left on the clipboard item

Delete manually by swiping to the right of the clipboard item, OR, in settings configure Auto Delete.

Search function available to narrow down a list of items, in case your list is too large
