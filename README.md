Ever wanted to have a history of what you copy to the clipboard for later use?
Multiple items stored away...text, Images, URL's Files...simply "share" and select the AiCopyPaste extension
Then swap to your AiCopyPaste app and select any item you want returned immediately to your current clipboard for immediate use.

You can select to store to iCloud and then have access to clipboard items amongst your devices.

Also can enable security locking via a PIN and use of Apples own biometrics to ensure no unauthorized person is looking at any clippings.
